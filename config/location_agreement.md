# Using your location in iScale

iScale can use your phone's or tablet's location to place potholes more precisely. **It is optional.** If you decline, everything else in iScale works the same: you pin potholes by tapping the map, and recorded drives are scaled from the camera height instead of GPS. Nobody, including your company admin, can accept for you.

## What iScale collects
- Your device's GPS position (latitude and longitude), how accurate it is in metres, and the time.
- During a drive you record in iScale: a GPS track, about one position a second, linked to that video.
- Only while iScale is open on your screen. iScale does not track you when it is closed or in the background.

## What it is used for (legal basis: your consent, PDPA section 19)
1. **"You are here" on the map.** Shown only to you, on your own device. It is not sent to the server.
2. **Pin where I stand.** The pothole pin is saved at your GPS position, with its accuracy. Everyone on this server can see pins, so they can see that someone was at that spot when the pin was made, and who made it.
3. **Record a drive.** The GPS track is saved with the video. It places the 3D survey on the map and gives the potholes real sizes. People in your company who can open the project can see the survey.
4. **Share location in chat.** When you press Share location, your position is sent once, end-to-end encrypted, to the people in that chat.

iScale does not use your location for anything else: not for marketing, and not to check your work or your hours. Your live position is never shown to colleagues or your admin.

## What a GPS track can show
A drive's track shows where you were and when, for the length of that drive.

## Who receives it, and where it is kept
- It is kept on this iScale server, run by **{controller}**.
- To show a pin's street address and road type, the pin's coordinates, without your name, are sent to OpenStreetMap's services (Nominatim and Overpass, run from Europe). If your admin has turned on live traffic, they are also sent to Google Maps. Your GPS tracks and live position are not sent to them.
- Authorities can receive data only when a law requires it.

## How long it is kept
- A pin's position stays as long as the pin exists. The person who pinned it or an admin can remove it.
- A drive's GPS track stays with its survey until you delete it (Settings, Location, Delete my GPS tracks), you turn location off, or the survey is deleted.
- iScale keeps a record of your answers to this agreement (accept, decline, turn off, with the date) for as long as your account exists.

## Turning it off
- **Settings → Location → Use my location: Off, at any time.** It takes one tap, the same as accepting, and works at once.
- When you turn it off, iScale stops reading your location immediately and **deletes the GPS tracks of drives you recorded**.
- Pins already placed stay as records of where the pothole is (for road maintenance), and 3D surveys already processed keep their road positions.
- Turning it off has no other effect on your account.
- Your browser also asks for permission to use location. You can block it there too.

- withdraw your consent (section 19);
- see and get a copy of your data (section 30);
- receive it in a usable format (section 31);
- object to its use (section 32);
- have it deleted or anonymised (section 33);
- restrict its use (section 34);
- have it corrected (section 36);
- complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee: complaint.pdpc.or.th, tel. 02 111 8800 (section 73).
